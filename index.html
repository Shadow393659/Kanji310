Kanji Visual Similarity Study Cards

Source checklist: the 310 characters in your lesson-by-lesson list. Grouping is based on visible shape, shared components, and look-alike forms—not meaning.

Each card keeps its original part and lesson so you can check it against your list. Readings are shown in hiragana. Each example is arranged as: Japanese word (with kanji), hiragana reading, then English translation. A kanji may appear in multiple groups when it shares more than one visible component or resembles more than one family. Repeated appearances are intentional.

Coverage note: the 310-character inventory is based on your supplied lesson list. Repeated cards across visual groups are intentional. Readings are shown in hiragana. Examples are common beginner/JLPT-prep vocabulary; JLPT does not publish one fixed official kanji list or a guaranteed per-kanji vocabulary list. Cross-check unfamiliar readings in a Japanese dictionary.
